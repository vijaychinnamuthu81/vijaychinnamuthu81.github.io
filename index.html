About me

I started as a SCADA and automation professional and moved into qualification and validation. That mix is what I bring to a project: I can read the electrical schematic, test the I/O, and write and approve the validation package that goes to QA.

For more than seven years I was validation lead at Merck Millipore on bioprocessing systems: the Cell Retention System range (50L–2000L) and its Bio4C ACE™ control software, the Smart Mixer and the Integrated Perfusion Bioreactor. I then led the full validation lifecycle for a HORIBA Scientific system supplied to Novo Nordisk.

I am based in Lille, France, hold an EU Blue Card, and work in English, with French in progress.
